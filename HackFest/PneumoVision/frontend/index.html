Pipeline Workflow

Pre-processing to Localization

A clinical-grade, 4-stage pipeline for chest radiograph opacity detection and confirmation.

01

Ingest Radiograph

Accepts DICOM (.dcm), PNG, JPG, or JPEG. Auto-detects photometric interpretation (MONOCHROME1 vs MONOCHROME2).

02

Percentile Normalization

Applies 1st & 99th percentile clipping to eliminate artifacts and standardizes contrast dynamic range to [0, 1].

03

Dual-Engine Inference

Faster R-CNN localizes focal opacities (512x512) while DenseNet-121 validates global pneumonia characteristics (224x224).

04

Coordinate Rescaling

Scales bounding boxes back to original patient resolution, maps anatomical lung zones, and prepares formal clinical reports.
